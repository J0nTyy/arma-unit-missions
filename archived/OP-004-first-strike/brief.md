# Operation First Strike

## Situation

AAF forces maintain a lightly defended communications outpost at Camp
Rogain, central Stratis. It relays targeting data to artillery positions
in the north and has been flagged for capture rather than destruction.

## Mission

Raid Camp Rogain, neutralize the garrison, secure the communications
equipment intact, and hold the camp until the relief platoon arrives.

## Objectives

- **Primary:** Seize Camp Rogain.
- **Primary:** Keep the communications van undamaged.
- **Secondary:** Capture the garrison commander.

## Execution

Approach from the treeline south of the camp in two squads, suppress from
the ridge, assault through the eastern gate, then set a hasty defence
against the expected counterattack from Air Station Mike-26.

## Extraction

None — the mission ends when the relief platoon takes over the camp.

## Notes for Mission Makers

Retired mission, kept as a reference for a simple two-squad raid layout.
The counterattack trigger fires 10 minutes after the camp is secured.
